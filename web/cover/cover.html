The cover · where the ladder stands

Five games between here and a Drawmaha solver.

Drawmaha is a split-pot draw/Omaha hybrid with no existing solver. So it gets built rung by rung: every rung is a smaller game whose answer is already known, and nothing moves up until the solver reproduces it. Three rungs are climbed and checked; the fourth is under way.
