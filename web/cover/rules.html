Rules

Five cards, one draw, and a pot that pays twice.

Drawmaha is a split-pot draw and Omaha hybrid, played here heads-up and pot-limit. Each player holds five cards, a board is dealt as in Omaha with one draw after the flop, and at showdown the same five cards are read twice: half the pot to the best Omaha hand, half to the best five-card draw hand.

One hand, read twice

The same five cards make a king-high straight flush one way and a pair of sevens the other

Dealt A♠ K♠ 7♥ 7♦ 3♣. The flop comes Q♠ J♠ 7♣; the 3♣ is thrown and the 10♠ drawn; the turn is the 4♦ and the river the 9♠.

Why it is unsolved

Two rankings on one pot, and a draw that multiplies the tree

2,598,960five-card holdings a player can be dealt, against 1,326 in hold'em and 270,725 in Omaha
32ways to discard from each holding at the draw, each opening onto fresh cards off the deck
2rankings on one pot: an Omaha hand and a draw hand, read from the same five cards

The solver works up to the full game through smaller ones with known answers. Mini-drawmaha keeps the draw and the split pot on three cards and a fifteen-card deck, and it is solved: read the research, see the benchmarks, or play the solver.

A research project by Alec Ondo. A heads-up pot-limit Drawmaha solver, built one solved game at a time, each graded against a known answer. Python, NumPy and torch; training on Modal; the site on Vercel. The code, the graders and the data behind every figure are public.
