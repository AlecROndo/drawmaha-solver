Benchmarks

Four games, each solved and graded against a known answer.

Rock-paper-scissors, Kuhn poker, Leduc poker and mini-drawmaha, in the order they were built. Each has an exact answer or an outside engine to grade against; the next game was not started until the previous one passed.

The results table, each game's convergence chart and its grade, and the mini-drawmaha and Deep CFR summary are being laid out on this page. Until then the live demos are at rock-paper-scissors, Kuhn, Leduc and mini-drawmaha.

A research project by Alec Ondo. A heads-up pot-limit Drawmaha solver, built as a ladder of games each graded against a known answer. Python, NumPy and torch; training on Modal; the site on Vercel. The code, the graders and the data behind every figure are public.
