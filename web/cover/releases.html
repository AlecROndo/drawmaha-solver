Releases

Every strategy release, with its grade and the command that produced it.

A release is a frozen strategy the site deals from: the tabular answer, the Deep CFR policy and its exact average, each graded by the same best-response walk.

The release table is being laid out on this page. Until then the strategy packs are published on GitHub.

A research project by Alec Ondo. A heads-up pot-limit Drawmaha solver, built as a ladder of games each graded against a known answer. Python, NumPy and torch; training on Modal; the site on Vercel. The code, the graders and the data behind every figure are public.
